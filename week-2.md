
# Week 2 – Gospel Connection: Personal Record Keeping Plan

## 1. Nephite Records

### What blessings did the Nephites enjoy because these records were kept?

The Nephites were blessed with the ability to preserve their spiritual history, God's commandments, and the teachings of the prophets for future generations. In 1 Nephi 4:13–17, Nephi recognized that preserving the sacred records was essential to protecting his people's faith and helping them remain obedient to God. These records allowed future generations to learn from their ancestors' experiences, understand God's promises, and maintain their religious identity. Without these records, important spiritual knowledge could have been lost over time.

### What blessings do you enjoy because the Nephites kept these records, and Joseph Smith brought them forth to our people?

Because the Nephites preserved their records and Joseph Smith translated the Book of Mormon, I have access to another testament of Jesus Christ. The Book of Mormon helps me strengthen my faith, understand God's plan, and make better decisions in my daily life. As taught in 2 Nephi 28:2, the things written in the book are of great worth to future generations. I am grateful that these sacred records were preserved because they provide spiritual guidance and remind me that God cares about His children throughout history.

## 2. Creating Your Goal

### My Five-Year Goal

Within the next five years, I want to complete my Software Engineering degree, establish a successful career as a software engineer, and develop advanced skills in artificial intelligence. I hope to use my technical knowledge to create meaningful software applications that solve real-world problems and improve people's lives.

### Discover – What do I need to work on?

I recognize that achieving this goal requires more than completing my college courses. I need to strengthen my programming skills, gain practical experience, improve my communication and teamwork abilities, and continue learning about emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence. I also need to develop better time management and maintain a healthy balance between my education, career, personal life, and spiritual growth.

### Plan – How will I accomplish my goal?

I will divide my five-year goal into smaller, measurable steps:

- **Year 1:** Complete my Software Engineering degree, strengthen my professional portfolio, and prepare for employment opportunities.
- **Year 2:** Gain professional software development experience through employment or meaningful projects while improving my technical and communication skills.
- **Year 3:** Develop deeper expertise in artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and software architecture through courses, certifications, or advanced education.
- **Year 4:** Take on more challenging software projects, improve my leadership skills, and contribute to applications that provide meaningful value to users.
- **Year 5:** Establish myself as a capable software engineer with strong AI skills and evaluate opportunities for further professional and spiritual growth.

### Act – How will I put my plan into action in faith?

I will dedicate time each week to studying, coding, and improving my technical skills. I will build practical projects, document my progress on GitHub, seek feedback from instructors and teammates, and actively pursue professional opportunities. I will also pray for guidance, remain faithful to my commitments, and trust that God will help me recognize opportunities and overcome challenges as I do my part.

### Reflect – How will I evaluate my progress?

At the end of each month, I will review my accomplishments, identify areas where I need improvement, and adjust my plans when necessary. I will evaluate my progress through completed courses, software projects, new technical skills, and professional experiences. I will also reflect on whether my decisions are helping me become more responsible, disciplined, and Christlike. This process will help me recognize both my achievements and the lessons learned from setbacks.

## 3. Refining Your Personal Goal

To improve my personal record keeping and support my five-year goal, I will take the following three steps:

### Step 1: Maintain a Weekly Progress Journal

Every Sunday, I will spend 15–20 minutes recording what I accomplished during the week, what challenges I faced, and what I learned. I will also identify two or three priorities for the following week. This will help me recognize patterns in my progress and improve my time management.

### Step 2: Organize My Academic and Professional Records

I will use GitHub and organized digital folders to preserve my programming projects, academic achievements, certifications, and important documents. I will update my portfolio at least once a month and write clear documentation explaining the purpose and results of my projects. These records will help me evaluate my growth and demonstrate my skills to future employers.

### Step 3: Review and Adjust My Goals Monthly

On the last Sunday of each month, I will review my five-year plan and compare my progress with the milestones I established. I will record completed tasks, identify unfinished goals, and make realistic adjustments. I will also include a short personal reflection about how my experiences have strengthened my faith, patience, and commitment to personal improvement.

By consistently practicing these three steps, I can develop a reliable record of my personal, academic, professional, and spiritual growth.

## 4. Personal Reflection Paragraph

Writing down my goals and breaking them into smaller, achievable steps helps me become more organized, responsible, and intentional about my future. I believe keeping personal records is important because they allow me to recognize my progress, learn from mistakes, and remember how God has guided me through different challenges. Just as the Nephites preserved their experiences and spiritual teachings for future generations, I hope my personal records will someday help my children and future generations understand the importance of faith, education, perseverance, and hard work. I can improve my ability to set and achieve goals by making them specific, measurable, realistic, and regularly reviewing my progress. Writing my goals also encourages me to seek Heavenly Father's guidance and consider whether my ambitions align with His plan for my life. When I document my goals, actions, and reflections, I create a meaningful personal record that shows not only what I have accomplished but also how I have grown spiritually and professionally. I believe this practice will help me become a better disciple of Jesus Christ while preparing for a purposeful and productive future.
